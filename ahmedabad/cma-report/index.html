A CMA report for an Ahmedabad bank branch

Businesses in Ahmedabad apply for working capital limits and term loans at public sector banks, private banks and co-operative banks. Each bank has its own CMA format and its own way of reading the numbers, so the first step is always to ask your branch which format it wants and for how many years of projections.

A CMA report is the set of statements a credit officer uses to judge a loan: past results, the current-year estimate, projections, the working capital assessment and ratios. We prepare it so that the numbers reconcile with your balance sheet and so that you can explain every assumption when the officer asks.

What Gujarat borrowers usually need it for

Many manufacturers around Naroda, Vatva, Odhav, Changodar and Sanand need a cash credit or overdraft limit to finance stock and debtors, or a term loan for machinery. Traders and textile businesses in the older parts of the city often renew or enhance limits each year and need the data updated.

Micro and small enterprises may also look at credit guarantee cover for collateral-free loans and at state and central MSME support schemes. Scheme terms change, so we check the current notification before we build it into your plan, and we do not promise approval or a scheme benefit.

How we work with you locally

You can send documents online and speak to us by phone or WhatsApp, or visit our Vejalpur or Naroda office to go through the figures in person. We confirm the format, agree the scope in writing, prepare the data and then walk you through it, so that you are comfortable before you meet the bank.

We prepare the CMA data and projections. We do not sanction loans, and the decision rests with the bank. Where the bank needs a Chartered Accountant's certificate for a figure, we coordinate it through licensed partner professionals.

Before you approach the bank

Bring your last two to three years of financial statements, provisional figures for the current year, the loan amount and purpose, and your stock and debtor details. Check that your GST returns, income tax returns and balance sheet tell the same story, since a credit officer will compare them.

Local questions

Which banks in Ahmedabad ask for a CMA report?

Public sector, private and co-operative banks all ask for CMA data for working capital limits and larger term loans. Each has its own format, so confirm it with your branch.

Can you prepare the CMA in my bank's format?

Yes. We confirm the format with you first and prepare the data to match it.

Do I need a CMA report for a small loan?

Not always. Smaller loans may need only simple statements. Your bank will tell you what it needs.

How long does a CMA report take?

It depends on how complete your documents are and the number of projection years. We give an expected timeline in the written scope.
