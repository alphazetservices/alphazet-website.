Registering a company from Ahmedabad

A company registered with an Ahmedabad address falls under the Registrar of Companies (ROC) for Gujarat, which sits in Ahmedabad. Your incorporation application, name approval and later annual filings are all handled through that ROC, online on the MCA portal.

That matters in practice because queries on an application come from the ROC, not from us. We read the query, correct the file and resubmit, and tell you in writing what was asked and what we changed.

Most founders we meet in Ahmedabad are choosing between a Private Limited company, an LLP and a One Person Company. A Private Limited company suits you if you plan to take outside investment or add co-founders. An LLP has fewer annual filings and suits professional and service firms. A One Person Company suits a solo founder who wants limited liability. Our guide on choosing a structure explains the trade-offs in plain words.

Where we work in Ahmedabad

Our head office is in Vejalpur and our branch is in Naroda, so we are close to business owners across the city. We regularly work with businesses around SG Highway and Bodakdev, GIFT City, Naroda, Vatva and the Sanand industrial belt, as well as traders and professional firms in the older parts of the city.

Most of the work is online, so you do not have to travel. If you prefer to sit down with someone and go through your documents, you can visit either office. Call first so that the right person is free.

What you need before we start

For each director or partner: PAN, Aadhaar, a recent photograph, an email address and a mobile number linked to Aadhaar. For the registered office: a utility bill and, if rented, a rent agreement and a no-objection letter from the owner. We list anything missing before we file, so there are no surprises later.

Government fees and stamp duty depend on your authorised capital and on Gujarat stamp rules, and they are paid to the authorities. We show them separately from our professional fee in the written proposal.

Local questions

Which ROC will my Ahmedabad company come under?

A company with its registered office in Gujarat is registered with the Registrar of Companies for Gujarat, which is located in Ahmedabad.

Do I need to visit your office?

No. The work is done online. You are welcome to visit our Vejalpur or Naroda office if you prefer to go through documents in person.

How long does registration take?

It depends on how ready your documents are and how quickly the authority responds. We give an expected timeline in the written proposal, and we do not promise a date the authority controls.
