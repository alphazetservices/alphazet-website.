Eligibility checks, documents and bid submission for government tenders in Gujarat, run from our Ahmedabad office.

Where Gujarat tenders are published

State government departments, boards, corporations and municipal bodies in Gujarat publish many of their tenders on the state e-procurement portal, nProcure. Central departments and public sector units use the Central Public Procurement Portal and their own portals, and the Government e-Marketplace (GeM) is used for many purchases of goods and services by both state and central buyers.

Each portal has its own registration, its own digital signature requirements and its own bid format, so a business that bids on all three needs to prepare for each separately. We register you where needed and explain the steps before you spend time on a bid.

Gujarat-specific points to check

State tenders follow the conditions in each notice and the state's procurement rules. Look for the turnover and experience criteria, the definition of similar work, the EMD and tender fee, the bid validity period and whether the tender allows joint ventures or sub-contracting.

The state government has its own policy on exemptions for micro and small enterprises, which may differ from the central policy. We check the resolution that applies to the tender in front of you and tell you what proof of Udyam registration the buyer will accept.

How we help from Ahmedabad

We read the tender with you, build a checklist from the eligibility clauses, prepare the document pack and review it before you upload. We help you with the digital signature, portal registration, EMD arrangements and the final upload, and we keep your rates confidential.

You can send documents online or visit our Vejalpur or Naroda office. We do not promise that a bid will win. The buyer evaluates every bid on the conditions in its notice.

Typical bidders

Contractors and suppliers from Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar, Surat, Rajkot and other Gujarat cities who want to sell goods, services or works to government buyers, and startups and MSMEs that want to qualify for the benefits given to them in public procurement.

Local questions

What is nProcure?

nProcure is the e-procurement portal used by the Gujarat government and many of its bodies to publish and receive tenders.

Do I need a digital signature for Gujarat tenders?

Yes. e-tender portals normally require a Class 3 digital signature, and some need separate signing and encryption certificates.

Can MSMEs bid without EMD?

Many tenders exempt registered micro and small enterprises from the tender fee and EMD, but it depends on the notice and the applicable policy. Check each tender.

Do you work for businesses outside Ahmedabad?

Yes. Most of the work is online, so we help businesses elsewhere in Gujarat and India.
