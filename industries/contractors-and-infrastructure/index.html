Finance and Compliance Support for Contractors and Infrastructure Businesses

Contractors win work through tenders, and every tender sets conditions on turnover, experience and financial strength. A missing certificate or a weak balance sheet can cost a bid you could have won. We help contractors prepare the finance and documentation side so their bids are complete and on time.

Problems we see in contractors and infrastructure businesses

Bid capacity and turnover eligibility

Tenders usually set a minimum average annual turnover and ask for similar work done in recent years. Knowing your numbers against these tests before you spend time on the bid saves effort.

Bank guarantees and EMD

Earnest money, performance guarantees and retention money lock up cash. Banks need to see your orders and cash flow before they issue guarantees, so a clear order-book and cash projection helps.

Long payment cycles

Government and large private clients may pay in stages, with retention held back. Working capital limits must cover the gap between spending and receipts.

Documents that must be current

Registrations, certificates, audited financials and experience letters must be in order and valid on the bid date. We keep a checklist so nothing lapses.

What banks and investors look at

Tendering authorities look at your turnover, net worth, similar work, solvency and the documents you hold. Banks look at your order book, the pattern of billing and receipts, your existing guarantees and your ability to carry the project cost until paid. Showing a cash flow by project makes both readers more comfortable.

How we work with you

We read the tender document with you and list every eligibility condition: turnover, experience, similar work, registrations and financial documents. We compare these with what you hold and tell you plainly where there is a gap and whether it can be closed before the deadline. Then we prepare the documents, register you on the relevant portals and submit the bid with your approval. For bank guarantees and working capital, we prepare CMA data and a project-wise cash projection that supports your request.

Questions people ask

Do MSMEs get any exemption in tenders?

Micro and small enterprises may be exempt from earnest money deposit in procurement by central bodies, and startups may get relaxation on turnover and experience. Each tender sets its own terms, so read the document.

What is bid capacity?

Many tenders compare your annual turnover and existing work with the new project's size. The formula is in the tender document.

Can you file the bid for us?

Yes. We prepare documents, check eligibility and handle submission on the relevant portal, with your approval.

Do you guarantee that we will win?

No. Award decisions rest with the tendering authority. We help you submit a complete, correct bid.
