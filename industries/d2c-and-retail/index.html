Finance and Compliance Support for D2C and Retail Businesses

A direct-to-consumer brand grows by buying attention and keeping customers. The numbers that matter are what a customer costs to win, how much they spend over time and how much inventory you carry. We help D2C and retail founders put these into models, valuations and decks, and protect the brand name.

Problems we see in d2c and retail businesses

Unit economics decide survival

If each order loses money after shipping, returns and marketing, growth makes the loss bigger. A model that shows contribution margin per order and per customer shows where to fix pricing or cost.

CAC and LTV need to be in the model

Investors ask what it costs to win a customer and what that customer is worth. These must be built from your own data, not copied from other brands.

Inventory ties up cash

Slow-moving stock and seasonal peaks strain cash. A cash flow projection that includes inventory purchases shows when you need funding.

Brand protection

A brand name that others can copy loses value. A trademark application protects the name and logo, and a registered mark also supports a better valuation.

What banks and investors look at

Investors in consumer brands look at gross margin, contribution margin, repeat purchase rate, the share of revenue from marketplaces versus your own site, the cost of winning customers and the stock position. They also look at how well the brand is protected and how dependent you are on one platform or channel.

How we work with you

We begin with your order data: price, cost of goods, shipping, returns and marketing per order. From that we build a contribution margin per order and per customer, then roll it up into a model with inventory and cash flow. For a valuation or deck, we use the same model, so your story and your numbers match. If your brand name is not yet protected, we prepare the trademark application, and if you advertise on Meta, we set up tracking so you can see which campaigns pay back.

Keep these ready

  • Order-level sales data for 12 months
  • Cost of goods and shipping cost per product
  • Marketing spend by channel
  • Inventory and purchase plan
  • Brand name and logo files for trademark filing
  • Last two years of financial statements

Questions people ask

What is CAC and LTV?

Customer acquisition cost is what you spend to win a customer, and lifetime value is the profit that customer brings over time. Their ratio shows whether growth pays back.

Do I need a trademark before I launch?

It is wise to apply early. A pending application is usually enough to start using the symbol, and the decision rests with the Trade Marks Registry.

Can you run Meta Ads for my brand?

Yes. We set up accounts, creatives and tracking and report monthly. Results depend on the product, the offer and the budget.

How do you value a brand with little profit?

Usually with projected cash flow and a comparable cross-check, with the assumptions shown openly.
