Finance and Compliance Support for Food and Agri Businesses

Food and agri businesses face seasons, perishable stock and rules on labelling and packaging. Cash needs swing through the year, and the bank wants to see that you can plan for the peaks. We help with the finance side and with certificates that apply to packaged goods.

Problems we see in food and agri businesses

Seasonal working capital

You may buy most of your raw material in a short season and sell through the year. The peak need can be several times the average, so a limit sized on annual turnover alone may not cover it.

Perishable and fast-moving stock

Spoilage, shelf life and storage cost affect both margins and the value banks give to stock. Clear records of stock movement help your case.

Labelling and packaged-goods rules

Packaged commodities have to follow the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, and many food products need a licence from the food safety authority. We help with the Legal Metrology packer registration and advise where other approvals apply; the food safety licence is handled by the authority and licensed agents.

Valuing a growing brand

A food brand that starts to attract investors needs a valuation grounded in its unit economics, not a guess. The method depends on how mature the business is.

What banks and investors look at

Banks and investors look at seasonality in your sales and purchases, the shelf life and turnover of stock, the margin by product, the dependence on a few buyers or suppliers and the regulatory licences you hold. For a brand, investors also look at repeat purchase and the cost of winning a customer. Showing monthly, not only yearly, cash flow makes seasonal needs visible.

How we work with you

We ask for monthly sales and purchases, not only yearly totals, because seasonality shows only in monthly data. We build the projection month by month, so the peak need is clear. If you plan to raise money, we value the business with a method that fits its stage, and we explain the assumptions in plain language. For packaged goods, we help with the Legal Metrology registration and tell you which other approvals the authorities may ask for, so nothing surprises you at launch.

Keep these ready

  • Monthly sales and purchase data for two years
  • Stock statement with shelf life
  • Product list with labels and pack sizes
  • Existing licences and registrations
  • Supplier and buyer concentration summary
  • Last two to three years of financial statements

Questions people ask

Can a bank finance seasonal needs?

Banks may sanction a higher limit for the peak season or seasonal limits. The structure depends on the bank, and your projections should show the peak clearly.

What is the LMPC certificate?

It is the registration of a packer under the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, needed for many pre-packaged goods sold in India. We can help with the application.

Do you handle food safety licences?

That licence is issued by the food safety authority. We can advise on the financial and other registrations that go with it.

Which valuation method fits a young food brand?

A cash flow method with a comparable cross-check is common, but it depends on history and data. We choose after reviewing your numbers.
