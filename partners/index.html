Fees and payment

Partner pricing is shared on a 15-minute call, based on your expected monthly volume. Partner fees are quoted per case, in writing, before work starts. The scope, fee and payment terms for each case are agreed up front, and you set your own fee to your client.

Confidentiality

Your client's information is used only for the work you give us. We do not contact your clients or use their data for our own marketing. We are happy to sign an NDA on request.

Where a licensed professional must sign, we coordinate it. Read more on how we deliver and on our quality and data security practices.

Partner questions

Will my client know AlphaZet did the work?

Not unless you want them to. Work is delivered to you for review and you deliver it under your name.

How do I share a client requirement?

By phone, WhatsApp or email. We reply with a scope and partner fee within 24 hours.

Do you contact my clients directly?

No. Client details are used only for the work you give us.

Who signs work that needs a licensed professional?

You can sign work that falls in your own licence. Where a Registered Valuer, Patent Agent or another professional is needed, we coordinate through licensed partners.

Can I see where a client's file stands?

Yes. We share status updates on every case.

Is there a minimum number of cases?

No. You can start with a single case.
