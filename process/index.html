Common questions

What if my documents are incomplete?

That is a normal starting point. We list what is missing before anything is filed.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. The introductory consultation is free. Paid services begin only after you accept a written proposal.

Can you work with our existing CA or lawyer?

Yes. We share drafts and acknowledgements so they can review everything.

What happens if a filing is rejected?

We tell you the reason in writing, correct the file and file again.

Do you serve clients outside Ahmedabad?

Yes. Most work is done online for clients across India.

No. Approvals, licences and funding are decided by the relevant authority, bank or investor.
