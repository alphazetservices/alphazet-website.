What the fee depends on. the purpose of the work; the number of projection years or scenarios; complexity of your business; the valuation method and urgency.

Any fees charged by a valuer, bank or investor are separate.

Approval and disbursement are decided by the funder.

Questions people ask

How are your fees decided?

By scope: purpose, complexity, number of projection years and urgency. You get a written proposal before work starts.

Do you invent numbers?

No. We build on your financial statements and assumptions you confirm.

Can you guarantee a loan or investment?

No. Banks and investors decide. We prepare documents they can read and check.

Will I get editable files?

Yes. Models are delivered in Excel with working formulas, and reports as PDF.

Is my data kept confidential?

Yes. Your financials are used only for your assignment.
