What it is and when you need it

A pitch deck is the short presentation you send before a meeting or present in it. Investors see many of them, so it has to answer the basic questions fast: what problem you solve, for whom, how big the market is, how you make money, what you have achieved so far, who is on the team and how much you are asking for and why.

Most weak decks fail for the same reasons: too many slides, claims with no evidence, and financial slides that do not match what the founder says in the room. We help you decide what to leave out, state your traction honestly, and build the financial slides from your model so that the numbers stay consistent.

A deck is not a promise of funding. Investors decide whether and how much to invest. Our job is to make sure that your story and numbers are clear and credible when they read them.

Questions people ask

How many slides should a pitch deck have?

Usually 10 to 15. Fewer, clearer slides are read more often than long ones.

Will the deck get me funding?

No one can promise that. Investors decide. We make sure your story and numbers are clear and consistent.

Do you create the financial slides?

Yes, from your financial model so that the numbers match.

Can you work with a deck I already have?

Yes. We review it, tell you what to change and rebuild it if needed.

Do you invent traction or customers?

Never. We use only real facts you can support.

How long does it take?

Usually one to two weeks after we receive your inputs.

How are your fees decided?

By scope: purpose, complexity, number of projection years and urgency. You get a written proposal before work starts.

Do you invent numbers?

No. We build on your financial statements and assumptions you confirm.
