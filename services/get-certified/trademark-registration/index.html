Trademark Registration

Protect your brand name and logo.

Request a Quote WhatsApp Us Call 9099629227

Final decision rests with the relevant authority. We do not guarantee approval.

What it is

Registering your brand name or logo so others cannot use it.

Who needs it

  • New brands
  • Businesses launching products
  • Owners whose name is being copied

Documents required

  • Brand name or logo file
  • Applicant ID and address
  • Business registration
  • List of goods or services
  • Signed authorisation

Time taken

Filing takes 2 to 5 working days. Full registration often takes 12 months or more.

What you receive

  • Brand search report
  • Application filed
  • Filing receipt
  • Status tracking
