What it is and when you need it

Registering a Limited Liability Partnership with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs. Limited liability with fewer annual filings than a private limited company.

People usually come to us when professional and service firms, partners wanting limited liability, businesses not planning equity funding. If you are not sure whether this is the right step, tell us your situation and we will say so plainly.

Final decision rests with the relevant authority. We do not guarantee approval.

Timeline and fees

What the fee depends on. the type of entity and number of directors or partners; authorised capital, which sets government fees and stamp duty; how complete your documents are; any urgency or extra filings you ask for.

Questions people ask

Do I need to visit your office?

No. The work is done online. You can visit our Ahmedabad office if you prefer to go through documents in person.

Are government fees included in your fee?

No. Government fees and stamp duty are separate and shown in the written proposal.

Can you guarantee approval?

No. Approval is decided by the relevant authority. We prepare the file carefully and respond to any query in writing.

What if one of my documents is missing?

We list what is missing before we file. That is a normal starting point.

Can you work with my existing CA?

Yes. We share drafts and acknowledgements so your CA can review everything.
