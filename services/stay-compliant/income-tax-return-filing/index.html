Timeline and fees

Typical turnaround. Usually 2 to 5 working days after we receive your documents.

What the fee depends on. how many transactions, invoices or entries you have; the number of returns or periods; how clean your books are; pending or late filings to catch up.

Late fees, interest and penalties imposed by the authorities are not part of our fee.

Fees are confirmed in a written proposal before work starts.

Who does the work

Filings that require certification by a Chartered Accountant or Company Secretary are certified by licensed partner professionals.

Work that requires a licensed signature (Chartered Accountant, Company Secretary, Registered Valuer, Patent Agent, Advocate) is done through our network of licensed partner professionals. AlphaZet coordinates and remains your single point of contact.

Questions people ask

Do you remind us of due dates?

Yes. We track your due dates and remind you well before each one. We also need your documents in time.

Can you work alongside our CA?

Yes. We prepare the work and your CA can review it.

What if we have missed earlier filings?

Tell us at the start. We list what is pending and the likely late fees before we file.

Who certifies filings that need a CA or CS?

Licensed partner professionals certify them. We coordinate everything.

Can you handle several filings together?

Yes. Many clients ask us to run GST, TDS, ITR and ROC together on one calendar.
