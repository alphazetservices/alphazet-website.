ROC Annual Filing (MCA Compliance)

Annual company and LLP filings done before the deadline.

Request a Quote WhatsApp Us Call 9099629227

What it is

Yearly filings with the Registrar of Companies for companies and LLPs.

Who needs it

  • Private Limited companies
  • LLPs
  • Businesses with pending filings

Documents required

  • Financial statements
  • Director and shareholder details
  • Previous filings
  • Meeting details
  • DSCs of directors

Time taken

Usually 5 to 10 working days after documents. Due dates are fixed by law.

What you receive

  • Annual returns filed
  • Director KYC
  • Minutes and resolutions
  • Compliance calendar

Late fees charged by MCA are separate.
