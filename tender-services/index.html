What we help with

Nine areas of tender support. We prepare and file; we do not promise eligibility, selection or an award.

Tender Search and Identification

Find tenders that may suit your business and read them properly.

  • Help identifying potentially relevant tenders
  • Review of notices, eligibility conditions and deadlines
  • Plain-language summary of the basic requirements

GeM Portal Registration Assistance

Seller registration on the Government e-Marketplace.

  • Guidance on required business information and documents
  • Support with profile completion
  • Help with product or service listings, where offered

E-Tender Portal Registration

Registration on central and state e-tender portals.

  • Registration guidance and profile set-up
  • Guidance on digital signature certificate needs
  • Portal-specific requirements explained

Tender Document Preparation

Your company papers, organised against the tender checklist.

  • Organising company documents
  • Declarations, forms and supporting papers
  • Financial and technical documents you supply or approve

Tender Filing and Bid Submission Support

Technical and financial bids, uploaded with your approval.

  • Review of submission requirements
  • Guidance on the online submission workflow
  • Upload and submission support, with your authorisation

EMD and Tender Fee Guidance

Know what the tender asks you to pay, and what it does not.

  • Explains tender fees and earnest money deposit
  • Helps spot exemptions the tender expressly permits
  • Portal charges kept separate from our professional fee

Technical and Financial Bid Support

Bids built only from facts and figures you confirm.

  • Organising the technical bid
  • Financial bid papers using client-approved figures
  • Checked against the published checklist

Tender Compliance and Deadline Support

Catch missing papers before the portal does.

  • Submission deadline tracking
  • Mandatory document review
  • Missing documents and inconsistencies flagged before submission

Post-Submission Assistance

Support once the bid is in.

  • Organising clarification replies and extra documents
  • Status tracking where the portal shows it
  • Administrative support for later tender steps, where offered

How a tender assignment runs

  1. 1

    Share your details

    Send the tender link or your business details.

  2. 2

    Eligibility review

    We read the tender and check eligibility and requirements.

  3. 3

    Checklist and fee estimate

    You receive the document checklist and a fee estimate.

  4. 4

    Prepare and review

    We prepare the documents and you review them.

  5. 5

    You approve the bid

    The final bid is approved by you.

  6. 6

    Submission support

    We support submission before the deadline.

  7. 7

    Confirmation and next steps

    We share the portal confirmation and what follows.

A bid is treated as submitted only when the portal confirms it. The accuracy of your information and the commercial terms of the tender remain your responsibility.

Fees, stated plainly

We do not publish fixed tender prices. Our professional fee depends on the tender's complexity, the documents required, the number of portals, the deadline and the level of support, and you receive it in writing before work starts. Scope can range from portal registration or a document checklist, to tender documentation and bid preparation, to end-to-end support within the agreed scope.

Tender fees, EMD, digital signature costs and bank guarantee charges are paid to the portal, bank or authority. We show them separately from our professional fee.

Tender questions

Do you guarantee a tender award?

No. The award rests with the buyer. We help you prepare and submit a complete bid; we cannot promise selection, and we are not connected with any government body.

Are tender fees and EMD included in your fee?

No. Tender fees, EMD, digital signature costs and any bank guarantee charges are paid to the portal, bank or authority. We show them separately from our professional fee.

How are your professional fees decided?

By the scope: complexity of the tender, documents needed, number of portals, the deadline and the level of support. You get a written quote before work starts.

Who is responsible for the information in the bid?

You are. Technical qualifications, experience, turnover and certificates must be yours and true. We never invent them. Final accuracy and the commercial terms stay with you.

When is a bid treated as submitted?

Only when the portal confirms it. We share that confirmation with you.

Can I send only part of the details?

Yes. Send what you have, even just a tender link. Tender portal, reference number and deadline are optional.
