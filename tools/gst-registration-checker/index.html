GST Registration Checker: Is Registration Required for You?

Find out if your turnover, state and sales channel require GST registration.

Answer all questions to see the result.

How this is calculated

  • Registration is required when aggregate turnover exceeds the threshold for your state and supply type.
  • Some situations require registration regardless of turnover, such as supplying goods to other states or selling through an e-commerce operator, subject to exceptions.
  • Thresholds are read from a rules file with a source and a checked-on date.

About this tool

Whether you need GST registration depends on three things: what you supply, where you are registered and how much you sell. This checker asks for your state, whether you supply goods, services or both, your aggregate annual turnover, and two situations that can require registration even for small sellers.

The basic turnover limits differ for goods and services, and they are lower in certain special category states. The rules file behind this tool lists each limit with its source and the date it was last checked, and it is shown under the result so you can see where the number came from.

Registration is generally required regardless of turnover if you supply goods to another state, or if you sell through an e-commerce operator, subject to the exceptions notified under the law. If either applies to you, the tool says so even when your turnover is small.

Even when registration is not required, you may choose to register. Many small sellers register to claim input tax credit, to sell to larger businesses that want a GST invoice, or to sell on marketplaces.

GST law has many special cases, including composition schemes, casual taxable persons and specific services. This tool covers the common questions only. If your situation is not straightforward, ask before you file. When you are ready to register, the documents checklist guide lists what you will need, and our team can handle the application.

When you enter turnover, use aggregate turnover for the whole PAN across India, not only for one state or one business line. Include exempt supplies and exports in the total, but check the definition in the law for your case. If you are close to the limit, remember that registration is required from the date you cross it, so track your turnover monthly rather than waiting for the year end. Late registration can bring penalties and tax liability from the crossing date.

Frequently asked questions

What is aggregate turnover?

It is the total value of taxable supplies, exempt supplies and exports under the same PAN across India, as defined in the CGST Act. Check the definition for your case.

Do I need GST for an online business?

Selling through an e-commerce operator generally requires registration regardless of turnover, subject to the exceptions in the law.

Can I register voluntarily?

Yes. Many businesses register below the threshold to claim input tax credit or sell to larger buyers.

Are thresholds the same in every state?

No. Special category states have lower thresholds for some supplies. The tool applies the limit for the state you select.

How current are the limits?

Each rule shows the date it was last checked and its source. Confirm against the official notification before relying on it.
