Tender Eligibility Self-Check for MSMEs and Startups

Check a tender's eligibility criteria against your turnover, experience and documents.

Documents you already hold

Fill in at least one requirement from the tender to see the check.

How this is calculated

  • Turnover: the average of your last three years' turnover must be at least the tender's requirement.
  • Experience: your years in business must be at least the years required.
  • Similar work: your largest comparable order must be at least the value required.

About this tool

Before you spend time preparing a bid, check whether you meet the tender's eligibility criteria. Most tenders ask for a minimum average annual turnover, a minimum number of years of experience, and at least one similar work of a stated value. Missing any one of these can disqualify a bid, however good the rest is.

This self-check takes the figures from the tender document and compares them with yours. For each criterion it shows a pass or a gap, and it lists the supporting documents you have not yet ticked off. The documents commonly asked for include GST and PAN registrations, an Udyam certificate, audited financials, experience certificates and a digital signature certificate.

If you are a micro or small enterprise, you may be exempt from paying earnest money deposit in procurement by central bodies. Recognised startups may get relaxation on prior turnover and prior experience. The tool shows these notes with their source and the date they were checked. Whether they apply depends on the tendering body, so always read the tender's own terms.

A gap does not always mean you cannot bid. You may be able to meet the requirement with a joint venture, by showing different supporting documents, or by asking a pre-bid query. Sometimes the gap is only a missing document, such as a certificate that can be obtained in a few days.

Use this tool as a first filter. Our tender team can review the full document, prepare the eligibility documents and handle registration on the relevant portals, so the bid you submit is complete and ready before the deadline.

Read the tender document line by line before you enter the figures. Eligibility clauses are often worded narrowly, for example turnover from the specific type of work, or experience in the last five years only. Enter the figure the clause asks for, not your total. Keep copies of the supporting documents ready, such as completion certificates, audited statements and work orders, because the evaluator will check them against the numbers you state.

Frequently asked questions

Do MSMEs get EMD exemption?

Micro and small enterprises are exempt from earnest money deposit in procurement by central ministries, departments and CPSEs under the cited rules. Other bodies apply their own policies.

Do startups get relaxations?

Ministries may relax prior turnover and prior experience conditions for recognised startups if quality and technical specifications are met.

Which turnover years count?

Tenders usually specify the financial years. The tool uses the average of the last three years you enter.

What counts as similar work?

The tender defines it. Read the clause carefully, because a narrow definition can exclude work that looks similar.

Can you check the full tender?

Yes. Send us the document and we will review the criteria and tell you what is needed.
